About Us

The story behind TechSaathi and what drives us

What is TechSaathi?

TechSaathi — the name comes from the Hindi word for "companion" — is a technology blog with one simple mission: making technology accessible to everyone in plain, simple English. We believe technology shouldn't be a privilege of experts. Whether you're a student, a shop owner, a homemaker, or someone who just got their first smartphone, there's something here for you.

What We Cover

  • Smartphone Tips & Tricks — Practical fixes for everyday problems: slow phones, dying batteries, and full storage.
  • Internet & WiFi — Boosting network speed, saving data, and choosing the right plans for your needs.
  • Online Safety — Practical guides on avoiding UPI fraud, fake calls, and scams, so your money and data stay secure.
  • Gaming — Mobile game settings, lag fixes, and performance tips for a smoother experience.
  • Useful Apps & Features — Hidden features in Google, YouTube, and other apps that make life easier.

How We Work

Before any article is published, we test it on real phones. If a step involves any risk — like a setting that could go wrong — we warn you clearly beforehand. In every article you'll find:

  • Step-by-step instructions without confusing technical jargon
  • Clear directions that work across phone brands (Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Realme and more)
  • Straight answers — no unnecessary rambling or filler

Get in Touch

Your suggestions are incredibly valuable to us. If there's a topic you'd like us to cover, or you spot a mistake in one of our articles, tell us via the contact page. We usually reply within 2-3 business days.

Thank you for making TechSaathi your tech companion! 🙏
