Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Thank you for visiting TechSaathi ("we", "our" or "this website"). Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Through the contact form: When you send us a message via the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message.
  • Through email: If you email us directly, we receive the same basic information.

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system (e.g. Android, Windows, iOS)
  • Your approximate location (country/city level only, never your exact address)
  • Which pages you viewed and for how long
  • The website that referred you to us

This information is used in aggregate form for analytics — it helps us understand which articles readers find most useful.

2. Use of Cookies

This website may use cookies. Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. They help us remember your preferences and improve your experience. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings — some features may be affected, but the website will continue to work.

3. Google AdSense and Third-Party Advertising

We may use third-party advertising companies such as Google AdSense to serve ads on this website. These companies may use information from your browser (cookies, IP address, pages visited, etc.) to show you advertisements relevant to your interests.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can read exactly how Google uses data from partner sites at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • For more details, please review Google's Privacy Policy and AdSense Programme Policies.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To improve the website and decide which new articles to write
  • To reply to messages you send us
  • To protect the website's security and prevent spam

We never sell your information to anyone.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so we cannot provide an absolute security guarantee.

6. Children's Privacy

This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent us information, please contact us — we will remove it immediately.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites — please read their own privacy policies.

8. Your Rights

  • You may ask what information we hold about you
  • You may request that your information be corrected or deleted
  • You may reject cookies at any time

To exercise any of these rights, reach us via the contact page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

10. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, write to hello@techsaathi.com or fill in the contact form.
