10 Hidden Google Features Most People Never Discover

By TechSaathi Team · Updated: August 5, 2026 · ~6 min read

We all open Google dozens of times a day, but most people only ever use it to search. In reality, the Google search box is a hidden toolbox — with everything from timers to translators to calculators built right in. After learning these 10 features, you can skip installing a bunch of separate apps. Everything below works right in your browser.

1. Timers and Stopwatches

Type "5 minute timer" or "10 minute timer" into Google — a timer starts instantly, no app needed. An alarm sounds when time runs out. Typing "stopwatch" gets you a stopwatch. From cooking to exam practice, it's always ready.

2. A Full Calculator — Scientific Included

Type calculations directly into the search box: 15% of 2400 or (45+55)*3 — the answer appears instantly. The calculator that opens below the result includes scientific functions (sin, cos, log, √). No need to hunt for a calculator app.

3. Unit and Currency Conversion

Type any of these straight into Google:

  • 5 km in miles
  • 100 usd to inr (live exchange rate!)
  • 2 cup in ml — extremely handy while cooking
  • 98.4 f to c — when checking a fever

4. Weather in One Word

Just type "weather" — you'll get the forecast for your current location. For another city, try "weather Delhi" or "weather Mumbai". The multi-day forecast appears on the same page.

5. Translate Any Text With Your Camera

Spot text on a board, menu or document in a language you don't read? Open the Google app → camera (Lens) icon → Translate. Point the camera at the text and the translation appears right on top of it — like magic. Incredibly useful for travel and study alike.

6. The site: Operator — Search Within One Website

Google's most powerful search trick. If you only want results from one specific website, type:

best phone under 15000 site:techsaathi.com

Results now come only from that site. The same trick is brilliant for finding documents on government websites: income certificate site:gov.in

7. Instant Word Definitions and Pronunciation

Encountered a new English word? Type "define serendipity" — you get the meaning, example sentences, and a speaker icon to hear the pronunciation. It works for words in many other languages too.

8. Reverse Image Search — Learn a Photo's Truth

Someone sent you a photo and you want to know if it's genuine or fake? Visit images.google.com, tap the camera icon, and upload the photo. Google shows everyplace the image appears on the internet. A powerful way to catch fake profiles and old news photos — also useful for avoiding online scams.

9. The Offline Game — Chrome Dino

Internet gone? Open Chrome — you'll see a small dinosaur. Tap it and an endless runner game begins! Space or a tap on screen makes it jump. Entertainment ready even without a network.

10. Flight and Train Status

Type your flight number directly into Google — say AI 202 — and get its live status (on time, delayed, gate info). Indian Railways trains work too: "12345 train status" shows live running status. Checking before you head to the airport or station is a smart habit.

  • define: — dictionary definitions on demand
  • sunrise / sunset — today's timings for your city
  • tip calculator — a calculator for splitting restaurant bills

Conclusion

The Google search box looks simple, but hidden inside it are a timer, calculator, converter, translator, dictionary and even a game. Turn even 2-3 of these 10 features into habits and your daily tasks get noticeably easier.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Do all these features work in a phone browser?

Yes! Timer, calculator, converter, weather, define — they all work in any mobile browser. For Google Lens, use the camera icon in the Google app or Chrome.

Does Google Lens work offline?

The Translate feature can work offline for some languages if you've downloaded the language pack in advance. Everything else requires internet.

How accurate are Google's currency conversions?

Google shows live market rates that are generally accurate. But banks and exchanges add their own fees, so the actual converted amount may differ slightly.
