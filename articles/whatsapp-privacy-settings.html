10 WhatsApp Privacy Settings You Should Change Today

By TechSaathi Team · Updated: August 29, 2026 · ~6 min read

Open WhatsApp right now and tap Settings → Privacy — what you'll find there surprises most people: by default, strangers who have your number can see your profile photo, your last-seen time, your status, and can even add you to any group. Your chats are end-to-end encrypted, but your personal details aren't automatically private. Here are 10 settings worth reviewing today.

1. Control Who Sees Your Last Seen, Photo and Status

Settings → Privacy → Last seen & online, Profile photo, and Status. Each one lets you choose Everyone / My contacts / My contacts except... / Nobody. The "My contacts except..." option is gold — you can hide from specific people (that one colleague, that group admin) without going nuclear. For a truly private setup, set profile photo and status to "My contacts" at minimum.

2. Turn On Two-Step Verification

Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Enable. This adds a 6-digit PIN that WhatsApp asks for occasionally and whenever someone tries to register your number on a new phone. Without it, a scammer who steals your OTP can hijack your WhatsApp; with it, they're locked out even with the OTP. This is the single most important privacy setting on this list — pair it with the habits in our online scam guide.

3. Lock the App with Your Fingerprint

Settings → Privacy → App lock → turn on Fingerprint/Face lock. Even if someone picks up your unlocked phone, they can't open WhatsApp without your fingerprint. You can set it to lock immediately or after a minute.

4. Lock Individual Chats Too

For chats that need extra privacy, open the chat → tap the contact's name → Chat lock. Locked chats move into a hidden folder that opens only with your fingerprint, and they don't appear in notifications. Useful for personal conversations on a shared or frequently-borrowed phone.

5. Stop Strangers From Adding You to Groups

Settings → Privacy → Groups → change to "My contacts" or "My contacts except...". This one setting ends the daily flood of spam and scam groups — random numbers can no longer drop you into "Earn money daily" or lottery groups.

6. Silence Unknown Callers

Settings → Privacy → Calls → Silence unknown callers. International scam call rings are constant these days. With this on, calls from numbers not in your contacts still show in your call list but don't ring. Genuine new contacts can still message you — and you can call back if the missed call matters.

7. Use Disappearing Messages by Default

Settings → Privacy → Default message timer → set 24 hours, 7 days, or 90 days. New chats will automatically delete messages after that period. Chat history stays for active conversations, but old clutter and sensitive forwards don't linger forever on both sides.

8. Hide Read Receipts (and Understand the Trade-off)

Settings → Privacy → Read receipts — turn off to stop sending blue ticks. The trade-off: you also stop seeing others' blue ticks, and this doesn't apply in groups (group read receipts always show). Many people keep it off for peace; it also stops "seen but ignored" misunderstandings.

9. Check Where You're Logged In

Settings → Linked devices — this lists every computer or browser where your WhatsApp is currently open. See a device you don't recognize? Tap it → Log out. Do this check after using WhatsApp Web at a friend's place or an office PC — sessions sometimes stay logged in for years.

10. Review Your Blocked List

Settings → Privacy → Blocked contacts. Scroll through it occasionally — it's amazing how many old numbers end up blocked and forgotten, and how many new spam numbers deserve to be there. Blocking a number hides your profile photo, last seen, and status from them instantly, no matter what setting #1 says.

Conclusion

WhatsApp's defaults are built for convenience, not privacy. Five minutes in the Privacy menu — two-step verification, app lock, group permissions, and unknown-caller silencing — closes the biggest gaps. Do it today, then help the elders in your family do the same; they're the ones scam groups target most.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

If I hide my last seen, can I still see others'?

No — it works both ways. If you hide your last seen, you won't see anyone else's either. The same applies to profile photo visibility and read receipts. WhatsApp keeps these settings symmetrical by design.

I forgot my two-step verification PIN. What now?

When you set it up, WhatsApp asked for an email address for exactly this situation — request a reset link via that email. If you never added an email and have forgotten the PIN, you'll be locked out of your account for 7 days after re-registering your number. Add the email now while you still remember the PIN.

Does turning off read receipts hide typing indicators too?

No — typing indicators ("typing...") show regardless of the read-receipts setting. Read receipts only control the blue double-ticks that confirm a message was read.
